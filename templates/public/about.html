About RxCare Hospital Pharmacy

Dedicated to precision pharmacotherapy, patient safety, and structured pharmaceutical care at Metropolitan Hospital.

Our Mission & Standards

Committed to Clinical Excellence

RxCare serves as the primary pharmaceutical care facility for Metropolitan Hospital at the VIT Chennai Campus Health Facility. Our team works hand-in-hand with medical physicians, surgeons, and nursing staff to ensure that every patient receives optimal, safe, and cost-effective medication therapy.

Unlike generic retail pharmacies, hospital pharmacy operations require rigorous controls across drug procurement, temperature-controlled cold-chain maintenance, unit-dose ward distribution, and comprehensive medication therapy management.

Core Operating Principles

  • Patient-First Dispensing: Prioritizing clinical appropriateness over commercial convenience.
  • Zero Tolerance for Counterfeit Drugs: 100% of pharmaceuticals are procured through authorized, licensed distributors with batch-level certificate of analysis.
  • Interprofessional Collaboration: Clinical pharmacists conduct chart reviews, dosage adjustment for renal/hepatic function, and drug interaction screenings.
  • Technological Integrity: Backed by enterprise Oracle Database XE 21c to ensure full transactional traceability from supplier receipt to patient billing.

Clinical Governance

Our Pharmacy Leadership & Workflow

How our hospital pharmacists safeguard every prescription step.

Step 1

Prescription Screening

A licensed pharmacist reviews medical doctor orders against clinical diagnosis, age, weight, and allergy history.

Step 2

Electronic Stock Check

Real-time Oracle relational validation checks batch availability, pricing, and ensures no expired medications are selected.

Step 3

Accredited Dispensing

Medications are retrieved from secure temperature-controlled storage, double-checked, and packed in clear unit envelopes.

Step 4

Patient Counseling

Patients receive clear administration instructions, potential dietary interactions, and advice on symptom monitoring.
