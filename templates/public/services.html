Hospital Pharmacy Services

Comprehensive pharmaceutical care for outpatients, admitted patients, and emergency departments at Metropolitan Hospital.

Inpatient Unit-Dose Dispensing

Admitted patients receive customized, pre-packaged unit-dose medications distributed directly to clinical wards and intensive care units. Every dose is labeled with patient details, drug name, strength, and administration time to prevent bedside medication errors.

Emergency Formulary (24 / 7)

Immediate round-the-clock availability for critical interventions: intravenous vasopressors, cardiac emergency drugs, antidotes, critical analgesics, and resuscitation medications stocked within the trauma bay and central hospital pharmacy.

24/7 Dedicated Staff Code Blue Support Rapid Dispense

Medication Counseling & Education

Dedicated consultation booth where clinical pharmacists explain medication regimens, proper inhaler techniques, insulin injection handling, potential side effects, and dietary precautions to ensure maximum treatment efficacy.

Confidential Counseling Drug Interaction Checks Chronic Disease Guidance

Temperature-Controlled Cold Chain

State-of-the-art medical refrigerators equipped with calibrated digital data loggers maintained strictly between 2°C and 8°C. Guarantees the potency of vaccines, insulins, monoclonal antibodies, and temperature-sensitive biologicals.

2°C to 8°C Storage Power Backup Cold-Pack Transport
