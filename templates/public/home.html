Clinical Safety Protocols

Patient Medication Safety & Standards

At RxCare, patient well-being is safeguarded through stringent verification at every phase of the dispensing cycle.

  • ✓
    Doctor Prescription Verification: Every prescription is authenticated against clinical diagnosis, dosage appropriateness, and patient history prior to dispensing.
  • ✓
    Expiry & Quality Assurance Alerts: Our Oracle database tracks expiry dates down to individual batches. Expired or near-expiry medicines are locked from billing.
  • ✓
    The Five Rights of Clinical Dispensing: Right Patient, Right Medication, Right Dose, Right Route, and Right Time are systematically verified for every order.
  • ✓
    Antibiotic & Antimicrobial Stewardship: We advocate responsible antibiotic usage. Medications are dispensed strictly for the prescribed course to prevent antimicrobial resistance.

Present Your Medical Prescription
